# Standards

Rules for measuring and reporting training efficiency that the project proposes for wider adoption. A standard starts as a DRAFT, is used and criticised in practice, and becomes ACCEPTED after at least one change cycle under [`CHARTER.md`](../CHARTER.md) §11.

| ID | standard | document | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| S-1 | Measuring and reporting training efficiency: compute at matched quality, FLOPs and time, strong baselines, pre-registration | [`METHOD.md`](../METHOD.md) | DRAFT v1.1 |
| S-2 | Standard baselines for the CPU harness | [`harness/baselines/`](../harness/baselines/) | DRAFT v1.0 |
| S-3 | Reproducibility: verifiable from logs, re-runnable, buildable | [`S-3-reproducibility.md`](S-3-reproducibility.md) | DRAFT v0.1 |

Comments on any standard are welcome as issues, especially from people who measure training cost in industry.
